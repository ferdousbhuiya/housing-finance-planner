# Product Roadmap

## Product principle
Every important input should answer: **If I change this, what changes financially?**

## Phase 1: Mortgage foundation
- Core mortgage payment formula and amortization
- Down-payment explorer
- Housing-cost composition
- Extra monthly principal simulation
- Responsive dashboard

## Phase 2: Purchase-cost engine
- Closing-cost categories
- Points and lender credits
- Prepaids and initial escrow
- Cash-to-close
- Conventional, FHA, VA and USDA assumptions
- Editable PMI / mortgage-insurance modeling

## Phase 3: Advanced payoff simulator
- One-time lump-sum payments
- Scheduled annual extra payments
- Custom dated payments
- Principal prepayment vs. recast comparison
- Payoff-date and interest-savings visualization

## Phase 4: Scenario comparison
- Save multiple properties
- Compare rates, terms and down payments
- 15 vs 20 vs 30-year comparisons
- Points break-even
- Sell-in-3/5/7/10-year analysis

## Phase 5: Accounts and persistence
- Supabase authentication
- Saved properties and scenarios
- Actual mortgage mode after purchase
- Cross-device access

## Phase 6: Regional ownership costs
- Florida-oriented editable fields
- Flood/wind insurance
- CDD and special assessments
- Condo/HOA costs
- Homestead/tax estimate

## Phase 7: Production
- Calculation tests
- Accessibility and mobile review
- Coolify deployment
- Environment configuration
- Stable release tagging
